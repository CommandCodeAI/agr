Agr plays Snake

Agr ranks directions and predicts whether each move is safe in one forward pass. In legal mode, game rules filter illegal moves and may override the top prediction to keep moving straight or break loops. Safety predictions are displayed and scored, not used to select moves. Top-prediction mode skips these rules except that reversing continues straight.
